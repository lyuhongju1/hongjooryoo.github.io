Research / Mind & Machines

Philosophy of Mind and Cognitive Science

I am generally interested in artificial intelligence as it connects to epistemic regress problems and applications for groundbreaking discoveries in science. With a collaborator, I critiqued a common conception of self-healing in agentic AI systems. Currently, I am examining machine creativity and the standards for establishing a result, particularly in experimental particle physics, where the deepest verifiable truths are to be discovered.
