Philosophy of Quantum Mechanics

I examine the foundations of quantum mechanics and assess its implications for the metaphysics of time, emergence, and ontology. In 2022, I investigated firewall problems and constructed the black hole final states in terms of Dr. Nomura's formulation of the black hole microstates (hard, soft, and far modes) at the Berkeley Center for Theoretical Physics as part of my physics honors thesis research.

I was funded by the Rose Hills Research Fellowship in 2022. See Publications for conference papers.
