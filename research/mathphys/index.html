Research / Physics

Mathematical, Theoretical, and Philosophical Physics

At Johns Hopkins, I am working on the foundations of physics, contributing to the development of theories of emergent spacetime and connecting various formulations of Hilbert space factorizations and quantum information to algebraic structures. I am also thinking about the role of topological quantum field theory and the theory of generalized stochastic processes in the foundations of physics and quantum gravity. These have various implications for the interpretations of physics and the emergence of time, which I explore in Explanation and Philosophy of QM.

I am advised by Sean Carroll, as part of a joint physics/philosophy doctoral program through the Interdisciplinary Humanistic Studies (IHS) department.
