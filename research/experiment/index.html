Experimental Dark Matter and Neutrino Research

At Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) with the Orebi Gann Group, I contributed to experimental research involving the Eos detector, the first optical detector to separate Cherenkov and scintillation light. My work includes PMT characterization, data analysis, and construction of the Eos detector. At Fermilab, I am also tangentially involved with the Accelerator Neutrino Neutron Interaction Experiment (ANNIE), where I use machine learning techniques to classify low-energy mu meson production.

I am funded by the Berkeley Physics Undergraduate Research Scholarship (BPURS) and LBNL.
