About

I'm a philosopher and physicist, and a PhD student at Johns Hopkins University (2025–2030). I was a theoretical and computational physics researcher in lattice quantum chromodynamics with N3AS and the Berkeley Nuclear Theory Group, and an experimental research affiliate with the Orebi Gann Group at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. I completed a quadruple bachelor's degree in physics, philosophy, applied mathematics, and cognitive science at UC Berkeley in three years, was a three-time recipient of the SURF Fellowship, and finished my honors thesis by my second year, alongside 12 PhD-level courses in physics, mathematics, and philosophy.

See Research for current work, Publications for a full list of papers, and Teaching for courses.
